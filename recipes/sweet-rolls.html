Sweet Rolls

Make soft buttery homemade sweet rolls with simple pantry ingredients.


Perfect as a dinner side or try your hand at some sandwich combinations with these delicious rolls.

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup warm milk
  • 1/2 cup warm water
  • 1 packet active dry yeast
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 1/2 cups all purpose flour
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp salt

Steps:

  1. Activate the yeast: Mix the warm milk, warm water, yeast, and sugar in a large bowl. Let it sit for 5 to 10 minutes until it becomes bubbly and frothy.
  2. Mix the dough: Whisk in the egg. Gradually add the all-purpose flour and salt. Mix until a dough starts to form.
  3. Add the butter: Add the 4 tablespoons of butter into the dough. Knead the mixture until it is smooth and slightly tacky.
  4. First rise: Cover the bowl with a clean towel or plastic wrap. Place it in a warm spot to rise for 1 hour, or until it doubles in size.
  5. Shape the rolls: Punch down the dough and turn it out onto a floured surface. Divide the dough into 16 equal pieces and roll them into smooth balls. Place them in a greased baking pan.
  6. Bake: Let the shaped rolls rise for another 15 to 20 minutes. Bake at 375°F for 25 minutes until golden brown.
  7. Finish: Brush the hot rolls with extra melted butter and a drizzle of honey.
