Sandwich Bread

Why buy bread from the store when you can bake fresh bread at home without all of the unknown ingredients and preservatives. After all, bread shouldn't last a whole month.


After baking your first loaf of bread you will fall in love, your whole home will smell like a bakery, and I can assure you this loaf won't last more than a week.....and not because it spoiled.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1/4 cup warm whole milk
  • 2 1/4 tsp instant dry yeast
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3 1/3 cups bread flour (or all purpose if that's what you have on hand)

Steps:

  1. Mix and knead: Combine the warm water and milk intoa large bowl with the yeast and a little sugar until the yeast becomes foamy (about 10 minutes). add the butter and salt and gradually add the flour. Knead by hand or with a stand mixer until the dough is smooth and stretchy but slightly tacky.
  2. First Rise: Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover and let rise for 1-1.5 hours in a warm place until it has doubled in size.
  3. Punch down the dough and roll it into a rectangle. Roll it up tightly to the length of a 9x5 loaf pan. Make sure the pan is greased or nonstick.
  4. Second Rise: Let it rise for an additional 30-60 minutes until the dough has risen about 1 inch above the loaf rim.
  5. Bake: Bake at 350 degrees for roughlu 35-40 minutes or until the crust is a golden brown and the internal temperature reads between 190-205 degrees. Cool completely on a wire rack before slicing.
