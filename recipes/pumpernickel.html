Pumpernickel Bread

Ingredients:

  • 1 1/2 cups water divided
  • 1/2 cup yellow cornmeal
  • 1/4 cup molasses
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 1/2 tsp table salt
  • 2 tsp granulated sugar
  • 2 tsp caraway seeds
  • 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa
  • 1 tbsp active dry yeast
  • 3 cups pumpernickel flour
  • 1 cup whole wheat flour
  • 1 cup cooked mashed potatoes

Steps:

  1. Combine 1 cup water with the cornmeal in saucepan and cook over low heat until thick, stirring often (about 5 minutes). Remove from heat and add moalasses , butter, salt, sugar, caraway seeds, and cocoa. Stir to combine and set aside to cool lukewarm.
  2. put the remaining 1/2 cup water in a large mixing bowl and addd the yeast.
  3. Add the molasses mixture and mashed potatoes.
  4. Gently spoon your pumpernickel and wheat flours into the mixture. Mix and knead the dough at medium speed for 10 minutes.
  5. Transfer the dough to a greased bowl. Sprinkle the top with flour. Cover the bowl and let rise until the dough is not quite doubled, about 1 hour.
  6. When the dough has risen you will see cracks in the flour you sprinkled on top.
  7. Shape the dough into a loaf and place in grease 8 1/2" x 4 1/2" loaf pan. Sprinkle the top with more flour or brush with a mixture of 1 egg white beaten with tablespoon of water.
  8. Once the dough is shaped, slash the top in a decorative design, and bake preheated 375 degrees oven for 55 to 65 minutes until the center reaches 200 degrees.
  9. Store at room temperature, well wrapped, for several days.
