Cheesy Garlic Bread

Try this delicious cheesy garlic bread recipe at your next dinner gathering. Whether at home or at an event, this recipe is sure to have everyone's mouth watering.


This recipe can double as a pull apart bread dish or as good old fashioned dinner rolls. Whatever your style, this recipe is sure to wow. You can even use our sandwich bread recipe in place of the french bread and bake the cheesy goodness right in and pull it apart.

Ingredients:

  • 1 large loaf of french or italian bread
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened at room temperature
  • 2-3 cloves of garlic, fresh, minced (or 1tsp garlic powder)
  • 1 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
  • 1/2 tsp italian seasoning
  • 1.5 cups mozzarella cheese freshly grated
  • 1/4 cup parmesan, grated
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps:

  1. Preheat your oven to 400 degrees and line a baking sheet with parchment paper or foil.
  2. Slice the french bread in half lengthwise to create two long open-faced halves.
  3. Mix the softened butter, minced garlic, parsley, italian seasoning, salt, and pepper in a small bowl until smooth.
  4. Spread a thick layer of the garlic butter mixture evenly accross both halves making sure to reach all of the edges.
  5. Top with the freshly grated cheeses, presing the cheese down lightly so it sticks to the butter.
  6. Bake on teh middle rack for 10-12 minutes until the butter is bubly and the edges of the bread are golden.
  7. Broil for the last 2 minutes if you want the chese extra melted and browned on top.
  8. Slice crossways into thick portions and serve warm.
