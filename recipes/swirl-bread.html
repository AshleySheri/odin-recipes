Cinnamon Swirl Bread

This sweet cinnamon swirl bread is made similarly to a classic sandwich bread and a cinnamon roll.


Who doesn't love a cinnamon roll? Well now you can enjoy it for breakfast as toast, french toast, or even a delicious sweet bread sandwich.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup milk
  • 4 tbsp melted butter
  • 2 tbsp sugar (for the dough) plus 1/4 cup sugar (for the filling)
  • 1 large egg
  • 4 cups bread flour
  • 1 1/4 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp cinnamon (for the filling)
  • egg white of 1 egg to brush the dough so the swirl sticks

Steps:

  1. Make the dough: Mix the warm milk, yeast, and 2 tablespoons of sugar in a bowl. Add the butter, egg, salt, and flour.
  2. Knead the mixture for 5-10 minutes or until smooth and elastic.
  3. First Rise:Put the dough in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise for 45 minutes to 1 hour in a warm spot until it has doubled in size.
  4. Mix the Filling:Stir the remaining sugar and cinnamon in a small bowl.
  5. Punch doown the dough and roll it out onto a clean floured surface into a rectangle. Brush the top with a beaten negg white. Sprinkle the cinnamon sugar mixture all over the surface.
  6. Second Rise:Roll the dough into a tight log, about the length of the 9x5 inch loaf pan. Let it rise for an additional 60 minutes.
  7. Bake:Bake your loaf for 35-40 minutes in a 350 degree preheated oven. Cool in the pan 10 minutes before moving to a wire rack.
